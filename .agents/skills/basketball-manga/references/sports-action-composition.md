# Sports Action Composition Matrix

Action-specific visual direction rules combining real basketball biomechanics with anime/manga kinetic staging:

| Basketball Event | Optimal Camera Angles | Body & Ball Staging | Recommended Motion / SVG Effects |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dunk (`dunk`)** | `low_angle` looking up through rim; `dynamic_action` | Peak vertical extension, off-arm clear, defender compressed below | `radial` focus burst centered on rim collar + `impact_burst` + upward leap arc |
| **Jump Shot / 3PT (`three_pointer`, `midrange_jumper`, `clutch_game_winner`)** | Multi-beat: `extreme_closeup` (eyes) → `low_angle` (elevation) → `behind_player` (arc to hoop) | High release point, crisp gooseneck follow-through, negative space above ball | `stark_white_tension` during ball flight; sharp net-snap `SWISH!` burst on make |
| **Crossover / Drive (`layup_drive`, `dribbling`)** | `dutch_angle` (`-6°` to `-10°`); low floor-level `medium_shot` | Low center of gravity, sharp shin angle, defender leaning off-balance | `diagonal` tapered speed streaks + curved crossover motion ribbon |
| **Defensive Block (`block`, `defensive_stop`)** | `low_angle` side-profile collision at apex | Outstretched fingertips meeting leather, shock wave at contact point | High-intensity `radial` focus spikes + `impact_burst` + `SMACK!!` |
| **Steal / Fastbreak (`steal_fastbreak`)** | `wide_shot` horizontal strip or `dynamic_action` | Lunging deflection into full-stride open-court sprint | `horizontal` / `diagonal` full-bleed speed lines (`speed_void` background) |
| **Confrontation / Clutch Pressure** | Split `extreme_closeup` on narrowed eyes | Forehead shadow (`kage`), sweat bead on jawline, intense eye gleam | `dark_pressure` vignette + dense 45° screentone atmosphere |
